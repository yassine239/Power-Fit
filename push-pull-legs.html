SUCCESS TIPS

Rest & Recovery

Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night. Take rest days seriously and use active recovery techniques.

Nutrition

Maintain a caloric surplus with high protein intake. Stay hydrated and time your meals around workouts.

Form & Technique

Focus on proper form over weight. Record your sets to check technique and track progress.
